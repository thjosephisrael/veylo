## Slide-by-Slide Script & Speaking Notes

### SLIDE 1 — THE HOOK (30 Seconds)
- **Background:** Midnight Ink (`#111B32`)
- **Headline:** *Why is sending money across borders still harder than sending an email?*
- **Sub-statement:** A text message travels around the planet in a second. Your money still takes days to clear—and loses 8% along the way.
- **Thesis Bar:** `01 One Human Account  •  02 Instant Open Routing  •  03 1-Click Mobile Checkout`
- **Presenter Spoken Script (30s):**
  > "Good morning everyone. Think about the last time you sent an email or a WhatsApp message to someone on the other side of the world. It arrived instantly, and it was completely free.
  >
  > But try sending that same person $200. Suddenly, you're asked for 20-digit IBAN numbers, SWIFT codes, branch addresses, and you wait three to five business days—just for a bank to take an 8% cut.
  >
  > Why does moving money still feel like mailing a physical letter? Today, we're showing you how Veylo changes that forever."
- **Key Takeaway:** The friction of international money movement is an absurd anachronism in an internet-connected world.

---

### SLIDE 2 — THE PROBLEM: DISCONNECTED SILOS (20 Seconds)
- **Background:** Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`)
- **Headline:** *Money moves across borders. Today's payment systems refuse to talk to each other.*
- **Sub-statement:** Three disconnected walls trapping everyday money movement.
- **Visuals:** 3 wide, airy white cards:
  1. **Commercial Banks:** *The IBAN Nightmare* — You copy 20-digit numbers, double-check sort codes, and pray you didn't mistype a digit.
  2. **Closed Wallets:** *Walled Gardens* — If you use Cash App and they use Venmo, you can't pay each other. Money is locked inside apps.
  3. **Online Checkout:** *Exposed Card Numbers* — Typing 16-digit card numbers into web forms creates friction and online theft.
- **Presenter Spoken Script (20s):**
  > "Here is the root of the problem: financial systems were built as isolated islands that refuse to talk to each other.
  >
  > If you send money through a bank, you're wrestling with 20-digit IBAN numbers and sort codes. If you use digital wallets, your balance is trapped inside their proprietary database. And when you buy something online, you're still typing 16-digit card numbers into forms.
  >
  > None of these systems connect seamlessly."
- **Key Takeaway:** Banks, wallets, and checkouts operate as disconnected walled gardens.

### SLIDE 3 — THE PROBLEM: THE REAL COST (25 Seconds)
- **Background:** Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`)
- **Headline:** *It takes too long. And it costs too much.*
- **Sub-statement:** Everyday people and small businesses take the hit.
- **Visuals:** 3 massive metric cards:
  1. `2 to 4 Days` (Settlement Delay) — Bank wires crawl through chains of intermediary correspondent banks.
  2. `8.0% Fees` (Remittance Toll) — Sending money home to Sub-Saharan Africa eats up nearly a tenth in bank cuts.
  3. `3.5% Toll` (Card Interchange) — Independent merchants lose thousands every month to processing fees and chargebacks.
- **Presenter Spoken Script (25s):**
  > "And who pays for this friction? Real people.
  >
  > The World Bank reports that sending remittances to Sub-Saharan Africa costs an average of 8%—a massive, unfair tax on hard-working families supporting loved ones. It takes two to four business days for money to arrive.
  >
  > And small businesses lose up to 3.5% on every card transaction plus billions in fraud. The plumbing of global money is simply broken."
- **Key Takeaway:** Financial fragmentation extracts an unacceptable tax in time, fees, and fraud.

### SLIDE 8 — HOW IT WORKS: LIKE EMAIL FOR MONEY (30 Seconds)
- **Background:** Slate-50 (`#F8FAFC`)
- **Headline:** *How it works under the hood. Built like email: open standards, not private walls.*
- **Sub-statement:** Decoupling identity from payment rails via open internet standards.
- **Visuals:** 3 clean architectural layers:
  1. **The Address Book (Veylo Identity Hub):** Resolves handles to accounts and secures approvals via WebAuthn.
  2. **The Handshake (Open Payments Standard):** Discovers wallet capabilities and locks 60-second exchange rates.
  3. **The Highway (Interledger Protocol v4):** Streams money across liquidity connectors into national rails (Faster Payments / NIP).
- **Presenter Spoken Script (30s):**
  > "How does this work under the hood? It works exactly like email.
  >
  > If you use Gmail and I use Outlook, we can email each other because of open standards. Veylo does that for money.
  >
  > Veylo is the address book—it resolves your handle. Open Payments is the handshake—it connects the wallets and locks the exchange rate. And Interledger Protocol v4 is the highway—it streams the money across the world in seconds."
- **Key Takeaway:** Veylo combines Open Payments discovery with Interledger routing to make money move like data.
